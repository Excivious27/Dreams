# Dreams — Art Prompts 13: the book's creatures, places and the 12B redo

WHAT THIS COVERS: everything the penguin story now needs that is still placeholder art — Catkins and Cat-topia, the finargles and Fennimore, the crystal babies, the merkitties, the catship and the litter catapults, Mount Prismatica's summit and the crystal prison, the new items — plus a re-run of the 12B batch that came back as repeats (Coldy, Big Jim, Pip-Pip, Nova cards / icons / partner abilities / gate panels). Two batches of 6.

RULES THAT APPLY TO EVERY IMAGE: 1536×1024, 16-bit pixel art, top-down 3/4 view, clean 1px dark outlines, warm saturated Stardew Valley / Sun Haven palette, flat #FF00FF magenta background, no shading outside sprites, NO GRID LINES, NO TEXT OR LABELS anywhere, no watermark, NO LETTERBOXING (fill the whole 1536×1024). Character sheets use the GRID RULE: 64×96 cells at x = 24 + N×88, y = 24 + M×120 (16 columns, 8 rows), feet at the bottom of the cell, the character 56 px tall. LEAVE A CLEAR MAGENTA GAP of at least 16 px between every sprite; effects that belong to a pose stay INSIDE that pose's cell. Save every image under the exact file name in quotes and upload to `art/incoming/`.

WHO IS WHO (from the book): the STAR CRYSTAL is a she, a soft white-gold six-point crystal. CATKINS is the leader of Cat-topia: a slim dark-grey cat in a hooded midnight-blue robe with gold trim, calm gold eyes, a small crystal pendant. FINARGLES are black fuzzy creatures the size of a pumpkin, two round purple-glowing eyes, NO mouth, tiny stub legs; they talk only in your mind. FENNIMORE is their queen: bigger, a thin purple crown of light, longer fur. CRYSTAL BABIES are two-foot toddlers covered in pale-blue crystal shards, angry little faces, stamping; their LEADER grows to five feet. MERKITTIES are cat-mermaids in blue-green with glowing necklaces (Mer is one). The CATSHIP is a wooden ship shaped like a sitting cat, ears for sails. GRUMP SHIPS are dark iron hulls with red eye-lamps. LITTER BOXES are grey plastic trays with sand (yes, really).

Attach the penguin-arc character references (Merry, Coco, Mer, Coldy, Big Jim, Pip-Pip, Nova) to the prompts that name them.
