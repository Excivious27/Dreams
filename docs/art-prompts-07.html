Dreams — Art Prompts 07: worn gear on the hero

Paper-doll layers so every armor set, outfit, tool tier, hair and hat shows on the character in every pose. These are larger jobs (8 images per batch for 1-2). ChatGPT may need "continue with image 5-8".

BATCH 1 — Armor sets on-body (8 images: 1 per set, 8 rows each = helmet, chest, legs, boots, gloves, shield, cape, belt)

Make EIGHT SEPARATE IMAGES, 1536×1024 each, 16-bit pixel art, top-down 3/4 view, clean 1px dark outlines, warm saturated Stardew Valley / Sun Haven palette, flat #FF00FF magenta background, no shading outside sprites, no grid lines, no text inside cells. LAYER RULE: 64×96 cells at x = 24 + N×88, y = 24 + M×120 (16 columns, 8 rows). Each cell shows ONLY the equipment piece drawn exactly where it sits on a 64 px hero body in that pose (body, head and skin left transparent). The 16 poses in order across every row: idle down, idle up, idle side, walk side 1-4, walk down 1-4, walk up 1-4, run side. The 8 rows are the set's pieces: helmet, chest, legs, boots, gloves, shield (held on the left arm), cape (behind the body, drawn so the body overlaps it), belt.
