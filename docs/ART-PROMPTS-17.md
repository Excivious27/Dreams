# Dreams — Art Prompts 17: skill trees, the last stand-ins, and Prompts 13

WHAT THIS COVERS, in order: (1) Prompts 13 again (the penguin-book creatures and places: never came back), (2) icons for all 520 skill-tree nodes plus the Sun Haven-style skills screen, (3) the last 37 item icons, (4) the six street-thug types and the Big Grump, (5) the new screens (crafting, save slots, settings, the hack, quality stars), (6) the four ending paintings, (7) turf and heist pieces, (8) the President's new look, (9) the app icon. Art Prompts 16 is the other half of the job (ground, cliffs, stairs, beach, caves, battles, colonies, lieutenants, town projects, Marked, Gift effects, the chrome).

RULES THAT APPLY TO EVERY IMAGE: 1536×1024, 16-bit pixel art, clean 1px dark outlines, warm saturated Stardew Valley / Sun Haven palette (gangster-arc pieces in the dark underworld palette where it says so), flat #FF00FF magenta background unless the batch says fill the image, NO GRID LINES, NO TEXT OR LABELS OR NUMBERS anywhere, no watermark, NO LETTERBOXING. At least 16 px of clear magenta between separate sprites. All ages: no blood, nothing gory.

SKILL ICON GRID (batch 2): 40 square icons, each 112×112, in 4 rows of 10: icon N (1–40) sits at x = 24 + ((N−1) mod 10)×148, y = 24 + floor((N−1)/10)×148. Each icon is a small framed picture like Sun Haven's skill icons: a rounded square tile with a 3px gold frame, a soft coloured background in the ROW COLOUR given, and one clear object in the middle with a soft top-left light. Every row uses its own background colour; icons in a row look like a set. No numbers or text on the icons.
