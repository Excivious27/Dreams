# Dreams — Art Prompts 14: the farm, grown up WHAT THIS COVERS: every crop that still has no growth art, 117 of them: the original field crops plus the 111 new ones (vegetables, herbs, berries, orchard trees, tropical fruit, mushrooms, flowers and the wonder crops), their produce icons, and the new kitchen dishes and keg brews. Until these arrive, the game draws a simple plant and shows the produce icon when it is ripe, so nothing is broken; this makes the fields look right. Two batches of 5 images. RULES THAT APPLY TO EVERY IMAGE: 1536×1024, 16-bit pixel art, top-down 3/4 view, clean 1px dark outlines, warm saturated Stardew Valley / Sun Haven palette, flat #FF00FF magenta background, NO GRID LINES, NO TEXT OR LABELS anywhere, no watermark, NO LETTERBOXING (fill the whole 1536×1024). Leave a clear magenta gap of at least 16 px between every sprite. Save every image under the exact file name in quotes and upload to `art/incoming/`. CROP SHEET GRID (images 3–10): cells 64×96 at x = 24 + N×88, y = 24 + M×120 (16 columns, 8 rows). TWO CROPS PER ROW: the first crop in columns 0–5, the second in columns 8–13 (columns 6, 7, 14, 15 stay empty). Each crop is SIX growth stages left to right: SEED (a seed in a little mound of tilled soil), SPROUT, YOUNG, GROWING, MATURE (flowering or green fruit), READY (ripe produce clearly visible). The plant stands on the bottom edge of the cell, centred, on its own tilled-soil mound. Small plants stay small (a lettuce is 24 px tall when ready); trees and vines may fill the 96 px height. Keep the same soil mound in every cell so the stages line up.
