# Full-game audit, 2026-11-17

There were three passes:

1. **20-area code and data audit.** It covered story, quests, hearts, items/economy, farming, crafting, combat, mine, companions, pets, UI, input, save, multiplayer, world layout, interiors, art lookups, audio, perf/web, and the title flow.
2. **20-bot playtest.** Each bot ran 5 in-game days with its own save dir: 10 penguin-arc and 10 gangster-arc bots, covering every playable hero and every play style.
3. **Fixes.** They went out in 10 commits ("Audit fixes 1" to "Audit fixes 10").

## Result

| Check | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Smoke test | passed, but missed parse errors (`grep -q` under pipefail) | counts errors; 0 |
| Interior reachability (new) | 6 interiors had things you couldn't stand next to | 0 failures |
| Exits and spawns (new, `tools/exit_check.gd`) | 7 exits dropped you 14–31 cells from the way back | 0 failures across 57 links (both arcs) |
| Playtest | 43/43 | 43/43 |
| Story, mine, hearts, creator | pass | pass |
| Two-process multiplayer | pass, with known dupes | pass; world state is host-only |
| 20 bots | 9 "dialogue stuck" warnings, 3 script errors | 0 fails, 1 bot-behaviour warning; the only game error found (fishing during a scene change) is fixed |

`tools/check.sh` (run before every push, and in CI) now covers the smoke test, interior reachability and exits/spawns. CI also prints the web pack size on every deploy and refuses a deploy if the pack goes over 600 MB.

## What was wrong, by area (all fixed unless marked)

**Softlocks and story**
- Follow-up beats that fired during another dialogue were dropped. They now queue.
- Story bosses vanished on reload. Spawns are now saved and restored, including from older saves.
- The town intro played twice.
- `%partner` was blank when nobody was out with you.
- The final choice freed Fennimore even if you'd never reached her.
- The "hold B with a partner" hint was out of date: the Meow Pow Wow is now yours (♥ button or hold B).
- Long lines are now split for the phone.
- Several lines were rewritten: the photo choice, "Welcome aboard.", the partner scene (you no longer hear yourself ask to stay), and a softer "break" ending.
- The Catkins shop is now real, after the cave shakes.

**Data loss**
- Saves are now atomic, with a backup. A corrupt file is kept aside and the backup is restored.
- The game saves when the app is paused or closed, and saves on the destination during scene fades.

**Multiplayer**
- Only the clock, weather, farm, builds, permits and cleared tiles are shared. Guests keep their own money, quests and friendships.
- Beds wait for the host.
- The relay rejects bad codes, survives reconnects without ghosts and keeps the host role.

**Farming and economy**
- Rain now counts as watering, both on screen and for the can.
- Fertilizer now works (it was craftable but did nothing).
- Seeds with no crop no longer plant.
- Seed Saver finds odd seed ids such as the Dream seed.
- Frost kills crops even if they were watered.
- Buy prices can't go below the best sell price.
- Six villagers got real ♥6 quests.
- Likes and dislikes now point at real items.
- The keg makes wine and cider.

**Crafting**
- You can't re-craft a tool you already own.
- A job never runs past tonight.
- Interior "every day" loot refills daily (it was every other day).

**Combat**
- Enemies no longer spawn in walls.
- Kill rewards are set per enemy.
- Burn no longer stun-locks.
- Stomps are telegraphed.
- Damage numbers show the damage actually dealt.
- Chill slows your swing and Dazed throws your aim off. Both were in the data but not applied.

**World**
- Villagers no longer stand inside buildings, in the river or in doorways. Spots are resolved against the live map.
- Fixed arrivals: lake from the farm and from the forest, the meadow from the east, hideout, highway, docks and neon.
- The crystal meadow's east gate is now on the east edge.
- The clinic door returns you outside the door.
- The bed's lower half answers A.

**Phone and UI**
- An ability button was added.
- Taps under the controls are ignored.
- The menu no longer locks.
- Dialogue choices scroll.
- The HUD moves out of the way indoors.

**Title**
- Going back keeps your hero and look.
- Default names are clean ("Pip-Pip", not "Pip-Pip (Too Powerful Penguin)").
- Pressing Back while connecting no longer starts a game.

**Art lookups**
- Gangster-arc heroes are sized like heroes and borrow the farm hero's tool poses.
- Friends' puppets use their own arc's sprite.
- Seasonal outfit sheets (`npc_<id>_<season>`) and painted villager portraits are now used.
- Empty placeholder strips are skipped.
- Pose sheets at a different scale are normalised.

**Perf/web**
- Water draws only what's on screen and redraws at 8 fps (it was every frame), and its shore edges are worked out once.
- The texture cache is let go past a budget on scene change.
- Sound effects are cached.
- Two quick area changes no longer silence the music.
- Tom's upload folders are no longer imported (`.gdignore`).

## Still open

- **Web pack size, about 205 MB.** Phones download all of it before the game starts. The next step is to drop unused sliced art from the export. That needs a usage map, because many names are built at runtime.
- **Granite and Sel have no portraits.** They go in the next art prompt file.
- **Two `ui_test` checks fail in the harness only:** a dialogue mouse click and a door click. Both work standalone and on phones.
- **120 seed items have no crop and can't be obtained.** They're catalogue filler and now refuse to plant. Either make them crops or remove them.
