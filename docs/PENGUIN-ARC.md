# The Two Too Powerful Penguins — arc design from the book

Source: `reference/PENGUIN-STORY-by-Tom-and-daughter.txt` (Tom and his
daughter's children's book, in progress). Everything below keeps the book's
events, names and spirit, and fills the gaps the game needs. Where the book
is still unwritten (after the merkitties), the game continues it the way the
book is heading: up the inside of Mount Prismatica to the Dark Crystal.

## The canon, as the game tells it

- **The Star Crystal** (the game called it the Light Crystal; renamed). She is
  alive, she speaks, she is *she*. She escaped from the Dark Crystal's army and
  came down from the sky. In the game she comes down in **your field** — the
  farm is on the edge of the hidden penguin island, now a little valley of
  humans and penguins who live side by side (Dreamhollow).
- **The Two Too Powerful Penguins** are **brother and sister**: Pip-Pip and
  Nova. The brave two who touched the crystal. One is the hero you play (or
  you play a human and they are your partners); the other is always at your
  side. They can fly (in cutscenes), hold their breath in space, and learn the
  Meow Pow Wow.
- **The Grumps** are the Dark Crystal's army: anyone the Dark Crystal has
  squeezed until only the wanting is left. Star crystals he controls, animals,
  people. They can be turned back — never only killed — with the **Meow Pow
  Wow**: hands make a heart at the chest, rainbow hearts and cat paws fly out,
  grumpy hearts melt and they remember love and happiness.
- **The Dark Crystal** was a star crystal who thought power mattered most. He
  wants to take away all the love and happiness in the universe. He promises
  everyone a "perfect paradise" and a crown.
- **Celestica** — the purple-and-green planet with crystal rings. Inside a
  storm-cloud tornado: a tropical island like Hawaii full of cats,
  **Cat-topia**, and its cave. **Catkins**, the robed cat leader, teaches the
  Meow Pow Wow. The cave is warm, full of cat treats, scratching posts and
  flying cats. The **catship** is a spaceship shaped like a huge cat. Its secret
  weapon: catapults that fire **litter boxes**.
- **Crystopia** — the crystal planet; **Mount Prismatica** at its heart, so tall
  it reaches into space, and it can only be climbed on foot. Magic stops any
  ship from flying up it.
- **Big Jim** — the big friend who "couldn't let you do this alone", brings camp
  gear, roasts marshmallows, needs a winter coat, screams about mermaid tails.
- **The crystal babies** — the first guardians of Mount Prismatica, two feet
  tall, covered in crystals, cursed to say only "bee bop boo baby". Their leader
  grows to five feet when she commands. **Coldy** (Coldie) is her real name; she
  holds the sacred key to the heart of the mountain and joins the party.
- **The finargles** — the Dark Crystal's spies: black, fuzzy, pumpkin-sized, two
  eyes, no mouth, telepathic. Teen finargles (ten thousand years old) can
  paralyse with purple eye-light and teleport you into a crystal prison. Their
  queen-to-be was promised Mount Prismatica by the Dark Crystal.
- **The merkitties** — Mer and her mercat friends, glowing necklaces, hidden
  pools under the mountain. They make you invisible to finargles, and they sing
  you a mermaid tail: *anyone who has been given the gift grows a tail the
  moment they enter water.* The under-mountain ocean tunnels run to the very
  top.

## Embellishments (game-only, in the book's voice)

- The Star Crystal calls the penguins "my brave two". She calls you "farmer"
  the way the book's narrator says "the two too powerful penguins": fondly,
  every time.
- Catkins ends every lesson with "a-meow-zing". The cats of Cat-topia are the
  town's cats; Biscuit (the pup) is unimpressed.
- The litter box joke runs: Litter Box is an item; Grumps hit by it stop and
  hold their noses for three seconds; Big Jim refuses to carry them.
- Coldy's chant "bee bop boo baby" is her ability name; the babies stamp in
  rhythm when she uses it (ground shock in a ring).
- Finargles have no mouths, so their lines are shown in *italics with no
  speaker portrait*: a voice in your head. The queen's name is **Fennimore**.
- The merkitty song is the **Tide Hymn**; Mer hums it when she's happy.
- The mountain's colours change with the hour (pink, purple, orange, green),
  so the mountain map's backdrop tint follows the clock.
