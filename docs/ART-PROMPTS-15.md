# Dreams — Art Prompts 15: the workshop, the rocket, and the operation

WHAT THIS COVERS: the new craft stations and their four tiers, the goods they make, the potions, the vehicles and the rocket, everything that belongs only to the gangster arc (the press and its bills, street weapons and armor, the soda fountain, the laundromat, the Recruit Training Center), and everything that belongs only to the penguin arc (the Crystal Shaper, the Litter Works, Big Jim's Campfire, the Merkitty Pool, the Crystal Baby Burrow and the Meow Pow Wow Shrine). Also the two villager portraits still missing (Granite and Sel), and a full redraw of the heroes and crew of the gangster arc in a dark underworld style. Until these arrive the game shows crates with the station's name, and blank icons. Six batches: 5, 3 and 3 images for the workshop, then 4, 4 and 7 for the gritty gangster redesign.

RULES THAT APPLY TO EVERY IMAGE: 1536×1024, 16-bit pixel art, top-down 3/4 view, clean 1px dark outlines, warm saturated Stardew Valley / Sun Haven palette, flat #FF00FF magenta background, no shading outside sprites, NO GRID LINES, NO TEXT OR LABELS anywhere (no letters on signs, bills or bottles), no watermark, NO LETTERBOXING (fill the whole 1536×1024). Leave a clear magenta gap of at least 16 px between every sprite. Save every image under the exact file name in quotes and upload to `art/incoming/`.

STATION GRID (station sheets): 8 columns × 6 rows of cells 176×160 at x = 16 + N×190, y = 8 + M×168. Each row holds TWO stations: the first in columns 0–3 (its four tiers, left to right, plain → grand), the second in columns 4–7. Every station sits on the ground at the bottom-centre of its cell, about 96 px wide at tier 1 growing to about 150 px at tier 4, and every tier is clearly the same station, improved. No people.
