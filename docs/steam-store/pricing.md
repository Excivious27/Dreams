## Steam discount rules that shape the plan
- Launch discount 10-40% (Valve suggests 10-15%), 7-14 days, set before release.
- No other discount for 30 days after release; no price increase within 30 days of release; no discount within 30 days after a base-price increase (https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/discounts).
