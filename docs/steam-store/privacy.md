# Dreams privacy note (S1 P5, 2026-11-20)

Status: **staged**. Published as a page on the game's GitHub Pages site and its URL entered in the Steam store's
privacy policy field during the Steamworks session. If the co-op relay (server/server.js) ever starts logging or
storing anything, update this note in the same change.

Dreams has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and collects no personal data.

Saves stay on your device (and in Steam Cloud on Steam, if you leave it on).

Online co-op is optional. While you play together, the co-op server passes your character's name and look and the
changes you make to the shared world between the players in your room. It keeps them in memory only, never on disk,
and forgets them 30 minutes after the room empties. Like any internet service it sees your connection address while
you are connected; it does not record it. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

On Steam, co-op through Steam invites uses Steam's own networking and Steam's privacy policy.

Questions: through the game's Steam community page.
