# Dreams — Art Prompts 30: what the loop-11 audit found

WHAT THIS COVERS, most important first: the town's houses and shops redrawn sharp (they were painted small and blown up, so on a phone they look blurry next to the crisp people and trees), each with a matching night version; a proper mine entrance on the meadow; organic forest walls and distant hills for the edges of the farm and other maps; curved cliffs and two-tier plateaus with switchback stairs and long ramps (both arcs); bridges and waterfalls for a river through the farm; the lake's three buildings; farm buildings at a real size (and a farmhouse that changes when you upgrade it); the gangster districts' buildings at a real size.

RULES THAT APPLY TO EVERY IMAGE: 1536×1024, 16-bit pixel art seen from the top-down 3/4 view of Stardew Valley / Sun Haven (light from the top-left), clean 1px dark outlines, warm saturated Stardew Valley / Sun Haven palette (gangster-arc pieces in the dark underworld palette — charcoal, oxblood, navy, olive, tarnished brass, with neon accents — where it says so), flat #FF00FF magenta background, NO GRID LINES, NO TEXT OR LABELS OR NUMBERS anywhere (not under, above or beside any piece, and not on any shop sign: signs show a little picture instead of words), no watermark, NO LETTERBOXING. Never use pure magenta #FF00FF inside the art (neon pink must be a warm pink such as #FF5AA8). At least 24 px of clear magenta between separate pieces. All ages: nothing gory.

BUILDING RULE: every building is drawn at the EXACT pixel size given (width × height of the whole building, roof and chimney included). It is HIGH-RESOLUTION pixel art for that size, never a small drawing enlarged: every shingle, plank, brick, stone, window pane, flower box and hinge drawn crisply, with no blur and no soft edges. Front wall and door face the viewer; the roof is seen from above at an angle. The door sits at the bottom centre of the front wall unless it says otherwise. To keep it in proportion with the people (a grown-up villager is 56 px tall at this size), a front door is about 68 px tall and 44 px wide and a window about 36 px tall. The building stands on its own with only a soft shadow under it: no grass square, no path, no fence, no ground tile, no sky, no background of any kind around it.

DAY AND NIGHT RULE: each building gets its own row: the DAY version on the left starting at x = 24, and the NIGHT version on the right, starting 48 px after the day version ends. The night version is the SAME drawing pixel for pixel (same size, same position in its row), only a little darker and bluer, with every window and the door glass glowing warm yellow-orange, door lamps and lanterns lit and a soft warm spill of light on the doorstep. No dark sky or background at night either: still flat magenta around it. Rows start at the y given, bottoms of the two versions level.

FRAMES RULE: an animated building is drawn as frames side by side, left to right, each frame the WHOLE building at the same size and in exactly the same place, identical except for the part that moves (a wheel, a light, a sign). Frames start at x = 24 with 24 px of magenta between frames.

EDGE STRIP RULE: a strip must repeat with no visible join: when two copies are placed end to end (left-right for a wide strip, top-bottom for a tall strip) the drawing continues perfectly. The edge that faces the play area is irregular and natural (bumps, overhanging leaves, shrubs, ferns, roots), never a straight line and never a staircase of squares. The far side fills its box right to the edge, with no magenta inside the box.
