# Dreams — Art Prompts 18: everything new the audit found

WHAT THIS COVERS, most important first: (1) one clean, consistent sheet for your pup plus gear that sits ON the pup, (2) moving bosses for three colonies, (3) the last icons (new items, spells, mail and goal-line marks), (4) night lights for lamps and windows, (5) the Hollow Heart's people and places, (6) small fixes (the Wastes spire, extra select cards, outfit portraits), (7) the hidden chests, buried caches and journal pages.

RULES THAT APPLY TO EVERY IMAGE: 1536×1024, 16-bit pixel art, clean 1px dark outlines, warm saturated Stardew Valley / Sun Haven palette (gangster-arc pieces in the dark underworld palette where it says so), flat #FF00FF magenta background unless the batch says fill the image, NO GRID LINES, NO TEXT OR LABELS OR NUMBERS anywhere, no watermark, NO LETTERBOXING. At least 16 px of clear magenta between separate sprites. All ages: no blood, nothing gory. Characters keep their exact look from the attached references.

Character sheets use the GRID RULE: 64×96 cells at x = 24 + N×88 (16 columns), y = 24 + M×120 (8 rows), feet on the bottom edge of the cell, the character 56 px tall, 16 px of clear magenta around every sprite. Rows are read left to right; leave unused cells empty.
