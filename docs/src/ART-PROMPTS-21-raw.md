# Dreams — Art Prompts 21: what the loop-2 audit found

WHAT THIS COVERS, most important first: (1) house and shop rooms that look finished (wall tops, night windows, props that belong to each shop), (2) water buildings without painted water, (3) small story pieces (Biscuit the old dog, the gangster opening panel, a ticket booth), (4) a withered-plant fallback and a wind-up warning icon.

RULES THAT APPLY TO EVERY IMAGE: 1536×1024, 16-bit pixel art, clean 1px dark outlines, warm saturated Stardew Valley / Sun Haven palette (gangster-arc pieces in the dark underworld palette where it says so), flat #FF00FF magenta background unless the batch says otherwise, NO GRID LINES, NO TEXT OR LABELS OR NUMBERS anywhere, no watermark, NO LETTERBOXING. At least 16 px of clear magenta between separate sprites. All ages: no blood, nothing gory. Characters keep their exact look from the attached references.

Character sheets use the GRID RULE: 64×96 cells at x = 24 + N×88 (16 columns), y = 24 + M×120 (8 rows), feet on the bottom edge of the cell, the character 56 px tall, 16 px of clear magenta around every sprite. Rows are read left to right; leave unused cells empty.
