WHAT THIS COVERS (checked against the game on 16 Nov, after your seven new sheets went in): Merry, Coco, Mer, Coldy, Big Jim and the two Too Powerful Penguins are selectable in the penguin arc and walk, attack and faint, but they have no farm-tool rows (hoe / axe / pickaxe / watering / fishing), no swim, ride or power-up rows, no four-direction attacks, no select cards, and only Coldy / the penguins are missing most expressions; the two mermaids and the ghost need a way to move over land that reads right (a hover / tail-glide); the five are also meant to be partners you can choose at the farm gate, so each needs a companion ability pose and effect; and from Prompts 11 three small fix-ups remain (gangster special moves came back with their effects fused into the frames, the gangster portrait grid had no gaps, and the six tier auras merged into one).

RULES THAT APPLY TO EVERY IMAGE: 1536×1024, 16-bit pixel art, top-down 3/4 view, clean 1px dark outlines, warm saturated Stardew Valley / Sun Haven palette, flat #FF00FF magenta background, no shading outside sprites, NO GRID LINES, NO TEXT OR LABELS anywhere, no watermark, NO LETTERBOXING (fill the whole 1536×1024). Character sheets use the GRID RULE: 64×96 cells at x = 24 + N×88, y = 24 + M×120 (16 columns, 8 rows), feet (or the hover shadow) at the bottom of the cell, the character 56 px tall. LEAVE A CLEAR MAGENTA GAP of at least 16 px between every sprite; effects that belong to a pose must stay INSIDE that pose's cell and never touch a neighbour.

EVERY BATCH IS 6 IMAGES. Save every image under the exact file name in quotes and upload to `art/incoming/`.
