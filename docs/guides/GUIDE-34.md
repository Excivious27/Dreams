## How to use the new things (plain words)

**Decorate on a phone.** In your house, tap the Decorate button (or use the dresser menu). A grid appears. Tap Box to pick a piece, then tap a cell: the ghost shows green if it fits, red with a cross if it doesn't (the reason shows at the bottom). Tap the same cell again to place it. Tap a placed piece twice to lift it, then drag or tap where it goes. Turn rotates, Store puts it back in the decor box, Undo takes back the last move. Wallpaper and flooring change the whole room. Done closes. With a controller: D-pad moves, A places or lifts, B cancels, Start opens the box.

**The catalogue.** Menu > Book > Decor catalogue. Every piece you own is in colour, pieces you've seen are shaded, missing ones are a "?". Tap the chips to filter by type, rarity, set or owned. Tap a piece for where to get it. Pieces you've seen can be ordered for delivery the next morning. The Sets view shows how close you are to each theme set and lets you claim its reward.

**The wardrobe.** Hero tab > Wardrobe. Pick a slot (outfit, hat, accessory), try a piece on and see it on your hero right away. Clothes have no stats: they only change the look. The "Shows on" line tells you honestly if your hero can show it. Owning gear with a costume unlocks the matching look.

**Where decor comes from.** "Home goods" shelves at shop counters and mega hub stalls (they change every 3 days, rarer pieces weekly), crafting stations (workbench, sawmill, loom, kiln and more), chests, mines, fishing, bosses, the Rec claw and prize counter, heart perks and side stories. Decor never fills your bag: it goes straight to the decor box.

**Rarities.** Common, uncommon, rare, epic, legendary, mythic, and unique (one per game, marked with a star). Each has its own colour and number of dots, so you can tell them apart without colour. Unique pieces can't be sold, trashed or gifted.

## What to test on your phone
- **Decorate:** place, turn, move and store three pieces in your house; change the wallpaper; leave and come back.
- **Catalogue:** filter by rarity, then order a piece and check it arrives next morning.
- **Wardrobe:** put on a hat and an outfit and walk around.
- **Shops:** find a Home goods shelf and buy something.
- **Bag:** decor you get should never take a bag slot.
