## How to use the new things (plain words)

### Animation life (new this round)

**The Town Hall fountain.** The water now flows: light streaks run down the falling water, rings spread in the bowls and small droplets splash, all smooth and never looping back to dry. Hot springs, glow ponds, tide pools and every other fountain work the same way. In winter the fountain is frozen.

**Wind.** Trees, bushes, grass, flowers, reeds, flags, banners and laundry sway, each on its own beat. The wind follows the weather and season: calm on clear days, stronger in rain and fall, gusts in storms. Indoors and in caves there is no wind. Crops sway too, and part as you walk through them.

**Weather and sky.** Rain and storms fall as slanted streaks, snow in three layers, a blizzard streaks sideways. Petals drift in spring and leaves in fall. Each lightning bolt flashes once. The aurora is soft moving ribbons. Festival days have their own weather.

**Town, beach and farm.** Lighthouses sweep a beam at night, in fog and in rain. The water mill wheel and your windmill's sails turn (gangster arc: a steel windpump). Clock faces show the game time. Boats and buoys bob. Sprinklers spray in the morning. Stations smoke or steam while they craft.

**Festivals.** On a Lantern Festival evening lanterns float on the river and rise into the sky, then fireworks. At the Star Festival shooting stars cross the sky, and one is aimed at you when you make your wish.

**Magic areas.** Sparkles bloom and fade on the ground, light veils ripple across the sky, and motes drift (wisps, spores, embers, dust, fireflies, data blips at Star Station). The moon has a dusk sky and aurora. Colony crystals glow and wake with a burst.

**People.** Your hero breathes and blinks when standing still, and after a few seconds glances to the side. Villagers breathe, blink, work, wave and rest in turns, and turn to face you when you come close.

**Animals and foes.** Birds sit on the ground and fly off when you come near, then land again. Fish show as ripples and leap now and then. Rabbits and frogs hop. Farm animals blink, sleep at night and hop when you pet them. Pets sit and nap. Foes rest and breathe out of step.

**Reduced motion** (Options) calms all of this: smaller sway, no gusts, no flashes, no fish leaps.

### Ranching part 1 (from CONDUCT 11a)

**The animals.** Chickens (Hens in the gangster arc), ducks, cows, sheep, goats, llamas, pigs (Hogs), rabbits and bees. Each lives in its own home: chickens and ducks in the coop, cows, sheep, goats and llamas in the barn, rabbits in the hutch, pigs in the sty, bees in the beehive.

**Buying animals.** Tam's shop has "Animals for your ranch": sheep, goats, llamas and alpacas. You need the right home with room, and some animals need a bigger home first (goats, llamas and ducks need tier 2). Hazel sells chickens, ducks, cows and pigs, Posy sells rabbits and bees (pigs now need the Big Sty).

**Homes and tiers.** Build them from the Plans board with materials:
- Coop: Coop (4 birds), Big Coop (8, feeder and incubator, Building 3), Deluxe Coop (12, auto-water and heater, Ranching 5).
- Barn: Barn (4), Big Barn (8, feeder, animals can have babies, Building 5), Deluxe Barn (12, auto-water and heater, Ranching 7).
- Rabbit Hutch (learn the Hutch Blueprint in the Ranching tree) and Big Hutch (Ranching 4).
- Pig Sty (learn the Sty Blueprint) and Big Sty (Ranching 6).
- Beehive, Bee House (Ranching 3), Apiary (Ranching 6).
- Silo, Tall Silo (Ranching 2), Twin Silo (Ranching 5): 240, 480 and 720 hay.
Each home has a fenced pen with a gate. Animals go inside at night and in storms or snow, and come back out in the morning. Buildings have their own snow and night looks.

**Feeding, grazing and the silo.** On a dry day outside winter, grown animals graze in their pen and need no hay. On wet days and in winter they need hay: hold hay and tap the animal, or let the feeder in a Big Coop or Big Barn take hay from the silo (milkers are fed first). Scythe long grass for hay, or buy it by the handful. Tap the silo to see how much is left; tap again to take 20 out. When you arrive on the farm a note says how many the silo fed and who went hungry.

**Petting and friendship.** Tap an animal once a day to pet it. The hint tells you what it needs: "A: Clover (hungry)", "pet me", "goods ready" or "happy". A good care day is a pet and a meal, in either order. Friendship goes up to 10 hearts; a missed day costs a little but never resets everything. Animals never get sick and never die: a hungry animal just looks sad until you feed it.

**Goods and quality.** Goods are ready in the morning: eggs, duck eggs, milk, goat milk, wool (sheep and llamas), rabbit fur, truffles (pigs dig them outdoors, not in winter or rain), honey and beeswax, and feathers from roosters. Happier, better-loved animals give silver and gold star goods and bigger eggs and milk. Tap the animal to collect; anything left from untended days is collected on your next tap. The morning report has one "Ranch" block with what's ready, who was hungry, births and hatches.

**Breeding and the incubator.** Any two grown, happy animals of the same kind in a home that allows babies (Big Barn, hutch, Big Sty, Bee House) may have a baby overnight if there's room. No gender checks. Babies sometimes come in a rare colour. In the Big Coop, hold an egg and tap the incubator by the coop: hen eggs hatch in 3 days, duck eggs in 4. Babies grow up in about a week.

**Names.** New animals get a name; rename them on the Animals page (Party tab).

**Rehoming (never sold for meat).** On the Animals page you can rehome an animal: it goes to live on Tam's high pasture and you get a small thank-you (nothing for very young ones). No animal is ever sold for meat or harmed. Bramble can't be rehomed: she's a keepsake.

**The Ranching skill tree.** The 14th tree, in both arcs, with four branches: Produce (more goods, a chance of a second good, better prices), Quality and Care (stars sooner, missed days forgiven), Breeding (more babies, faster growing, a warmer incubator), and Friends and Helpers (petting counts double sometimes, Farm Hands). It also teaches the Hutch and Sty Blueprints. Care, petting and collecting give Ranching XP. Old saves with animals got some Ranching XP to start.

**New stations and goods.** Mayo Machine (eggs to mayonnaise, duck mayonnaise) and Honey Extractor (honeycomb to honey and beeswax, jars of honey, beeswax candles), each with 2 tiers. The cheese press also makes cream, honey yogurt and truffle oil. The loom makes yarn from 1 wool + 1 fiber (2 yarn) and from rabbit fur, and a Down Pillow from feathers and cloth. Honey in a keg makes Flower Mead (a trade good only, nobody drinks it). Starred goods keep their star when you process them. The Book has a new Ranch log.

**Team animals and lessons.** A grown animal at 2 hearts can learn lessons: on the Animals page, tap Teach with one of its treats (carrot, apple, hay, wheat or berry). One lesson a day, 8 energy. Lessons: Charge (butts foes), Guard (holds its ground beside you), Fetch (brings forage), Carry (hauls the harvest). After a lesson it can join your team (up to 2). Team animals follow you outdoors, help in fights and battle campaigns, and show on the Hero tab's Team card. If one gets knocked down it rests in its pen until the next day.

**Overnight chores.** From day 8, the Chores page (Party tab) lets your pup, team animals, crew and partner do one chore each night: water crops, harvest ripe crops into a chest, feed the animals, keep them company, or forage the edges into a chest. Each helper has energy and gets better with practice (5 levels). The Ranching tree's Farm Hands add two more helpers for feeding and petting. The morning report says what they did; a full chest is reported and nothing is lost.

**Bramble.** Reach 8 hearts with Tam and he promises you a lamb. Once your barn has room, Bramble arrives (rename her if you like). After 10 hearts Tam writes four letters from the highlands, and asks you to bring her to the Harvest Fair.

**Animal Show and Best Egg.** At the Harvest Fair (the Block Fair livestock ribbon in the gangster arc), enter a grown animal (friendship and mood count most) or your best animal good. At the Egg Festival, the Best Egg table takes your best egg. Prizes are money; your animal goes home after the show.

**Market stalls.** On the plaza, Benno's feed stall (9 to 11) and Hazel's farm stall (8 to 11) sell hay, seed and fertiliser. Shut on festival days.

**Letters and villagers.** Hazel (Benno in the gangster arc) writes about animals around days 6 to 14, welcome letters come with a small gift when you build or upgrade a coop or barn, and you get a letter for your first egg, milk and wool. Favour letters only ask for animal goods you can actually make. Your spouse feeds every animal on chore days.

### From CONDUCT 10b (still the same)

**Restyling the farmhouse.** Tap the Plans board and pick "Restyle the farmhouse". The camera frames your house. Tabs along the top: Roof, Walls, Windows, Stonework, Door, Shutters, Porch, Mailbox, Pen fence. Tap < and > to try styles; the house changes live. Buy pays once; after that Apply switches for free. Original puts the first look back. Closing without buying keeps your saved look.

**Decorating the yard.** Near your house, tap "Decorate yard" (bottom left), or use the Plans board. It works like decorating indoors: green ghost fits, red with a cross doesn't, and the reason shows. Paths and small pieces can be painted by dragging. You can't build on soil, doors, exits, the river, pens or building sites. If you start a building where yard decor stands, the decor goes back to the box.

**Tess's shop.** Tess is the carpenter in town. She writes to you around day 3. Her workshop sells furniture and yard pieces, and the shelves change every few days. Gangster crew counters (Marlene, Deuce, Specs, Halo) sell their own pieces too. Ordering a piece you've seen costs 10% extra (at least 10), delivered next morning.

**The casino and its limits (gangster arc).** The Gilded Ace is on Casino Row in Gilt City, open 6pm to 2am. Five tables: slots, roulette, Big/Small dice, blackjack and video poker. Every table tells you exactly how much the house keeps; every bet returns less than 99% over time, so the house always wins in the long run. Each table has a free practice hand. Limits: a $500 daily loss limit (on by default), a cap on hands per day, a reality check, and "Take a break" (7 days, can't be undone). Big wins add heat; with heat 3 or more the doorman turns you away. Keepsakes come for trying tables, never for winning money. The Odds board on the Row shows every table's numbers.

**The wardrobe on every hero.** At the dresser at home, or Hero tab > Wardrobe. Hats and face pieces show on every drawn hero, and capes show on everyone. Pieces that can't be drawn on your hero say so. Sets show a Claim button when complete. In co-op your friend sees your outfit.

## What to test on your phone
- **Town Hall fountain:** stand by it for a while. The water should flow smoothly and never pop back to dry.
- **Windy or rainy day in town:** watch the trees, flags and laundry sway out of step, and the rain fall at a slant.
- **Crops in a breeze:** walk through a planted field; the crops should sway and part around you.
- **Beach lighthouse at night:** look for one smooth sweeping beam.
- **Lantern Festival evening:** watch the river and sky lanterns, then the fireworks.
- **Crystal meadow at night:** look for ground sparkles, motes and the sky shimmer.
- **Your hero:** stand still for 10 seconds and watch your hero breathe, blink and glance to the side.
- **Villagers at work:** watch a few villagers; they should work, wave and rest, not freeze or twitch, and turn to you when you come close.
- **Birds and fish:** walk up to birds on the ground and watch them fly off and land again; watch a pond for ripples and a fish leap.
- **Farm animals at night:** visit the pens after dark; the animals should be asleep. Pet one by day to see it hop.
- **Beach:** walk the beach end to end: wreck, family beach, cove, marina and lighthouse point. Try it in summer and in winter.
- **Marina:** talk to Hollis on the main pier, open his board and build T1. Later, build T2 and take a boat to the marsh.
