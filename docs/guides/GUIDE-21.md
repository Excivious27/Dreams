# PART 1 — What changed, and what to test

## Smoother
- Walking around the Hollow Heart, Highlands and Ruins no longer hitches. Those spikes reached about 4,000 draw calls.

## Co-op
- While you visit someone, your crafting jobs, tool upgrades, haste and drone timers keep your own time.
- Closing the app mid-visit no longer leaves you with 35-day buffs.
- Guests can't make chests on the host's farm or take goods from the host's animals.
- If you get disconnected, you see "Reconnecting…" instead of being charged.

## Fights
- Enemies no longer get stuck on wall ends or in streams.
- Shooters stop jittering when they have no clear shot.
- Bosses and colony quota foes don't use up a map's 12 paid kills.
- A battle you haven't won pays for up to 80 kills a day, so losing on purpose doesn't farm crystals.

## Skills
- **Hot Loads** is now **Fresh Rounds**. Your points move to it automatically.
- Resetting Sorcery keeps the spells the colonies gave you.
- A hoe or axe swing no longer borrows your gun's damage.
- Skill tabs open at the top.

## Farm
- Top-tier station pictures fit their spots.
- Construction huts no longer block the road to the forest.
- Construction sites stay clear of rocks and trees.

## Regions
- Colony plateaus are visible, with their ramps. No more invisible walls in coral and kelp.
- The quarry's fake ore and frozen crabs are gone.
- The forest changes colour with the seasons.

## Story
- After the ending, the goal line no longer points at the beaten boss.
- Chapter 3 waits for the Commander to fall.
- 8-heart scenes wait until the 6-heart quest is done.
- Beating a district boss last still claims the block.
- 22 villagers have their own birthday line.

## Screen, rooms, sound
- **Screen:** toolbar icons keep their real shape, and USE, CHECK and MENU show their names.
- **Rooms:**
  - no black gutter bars on walls;
  - windows are no longer hidden behind furniture;
  - shopkeepers go off duty at night.
- **Sounds:** new sounds for the EMP, bots, drone, tonics and a full bag, a heart you earn anywhere, failed casts and boss moves.
- **Character creator:** an Outfit picker replaces the Body picker, and hair colours really change.

## Art re-cut
- **Item icons:** 901 icons are cleaned of slivers and caption bars, and the outfit icons sit in the right order.
- **Town:** the benches are whole.
- **Characters:** pets, hounds and golems are now one per frame, and Orla and Benno have new poses.

## What to test (10 minutes)
1. Open the shop and your bag. Do the item icons look clean?
2. Make a new character and try the hair colours and the Outfit picker.
3. Fight near a wall or stream. Do enemies still get stuck?
4. Visit the coral colony and walk up the plateau.
5. If you play co-op, visit someone, close the app, reopen and check your buffs.
