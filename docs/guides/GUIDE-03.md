# Guide #3: what happens next (and what I need from you)

**Where the game is now (16 Nov).** Organic regions with cliff/tree/water
borders, blob-tiled paths and shores, a redesigned forest, interiors for every
town and lake building, a 7-skill perk tree, ~775 items with rarity/tier, tiered
crafting at four station types, enemies with stat/drop tables, main-story quest
chains for both arcs plus townsfolk side quests, equipment slots with stats, the
tool-tier ladder at the smith, a tint-based look picker at new game, and 160 art
sheets sliced into ~6,000 sprites. Playtest harness is green.

**The one thing holding back the "look" of the game:** everything you equip is
tracked and changes your stats, but it does not show on the hero yet. Four art
batches came back as detached icons (armor, outfits, tools, hair, hats). That is
not wasted — the tool and hat icons are already in the toolbar/inventory, and the
hair/hat rows are pose-matched — but a reliable paper-doll needs the *body*
drawn in each costume. Art Prompts 08 asks for exactly that.
