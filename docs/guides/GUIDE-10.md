# PART 1 — What's new since Guide 09

## Skills, Sun Haven style
- **Layout:** each skill has its own tab. The panel shows your level, skill points and an EXP bar, then the tree as a grid: 4 branches (rows) by 10 tiers (columns I to X), with ranks shown on each node (like "1/3").
- **How it works:** every level gives one point. A tier column opens after 2 more points in that skill. Tap a node to read it, then tap **Learn**. You can reset a tree for free.
- **Size:** there are 13 skills and 520 nodes, more choices than Sun Haven. You can't take everything, so you choose a build.
- **The skills:** Farming, Mining, Foraging, Fishing, Combat, Sorcery, Pup, Bartering, Building, plus:
  - **Leadership** (both arcs): teamwork. It levels up whenever you work with your team: partner powers, fighting beside your partner or pup, crew jobs, capturing points, building helpers, and friendship with companions or crew.
    - **Command:** more damage and faster partner powers.
    - **Care:** heal when you use a partner power, and take less damage with a partner.
    - **Bonds:** friendship gains and pup health.
    - **Crew Work:** crew job pay, and more skill EXP while a partner is with you.
  - **Strategist** (both arcs): conquest battles. It levels up with every battle campaign you fight; a win gives the most, a loss still teaches.
    - **Ground:** faster captures and more starting supply.
    - **Logistics:** supply income and cheaper spires or drone bays.
    - **Forces:** helper health and damage.
    - **Command:** the enemy loses tickets faster, and you lose them slower.
  - **Harmony** (penguin arc only):
    - **Bond:** faster partner powers and health.
    - **Song:** mana and energy.
    - **Islands:** cheaper colony shops and mana.
    - **Wardens:** battle helpers and supply.
  - **Hustle** (gangster arc only):
    - **Street:** less risk passing bills, and heat cools faster.
    - **Money:** laundromat and crew job pay.
    - **Muscle:** gun damage and health.
    - **Operations:** supply, drone health and energy.
- **Safety caps:** effects that stack have ceilings, so nothing becomes free or certain.
- **Icons:** until the icon sheets arrive (Art Prompts 17, batch 2), the nodes show item icons.

## The President, rewritten
He is now **M.**, the person who got you out of the city with a coat over your head and a deed in your pocket, and the only one who knew you were alive.
- **The letters:** every letter you got from M. was him, written so you'd do exactly what you did.
- **The setup:** he sent the man with the photograph so you'd be angry enough to climb his stairs.
- **The crystal:** twenty-one years ago a star fell in your field. He took one piece and left the rest under your soil, for someone it would trust: you.
- **What drives him:** the stone promised him nobody would ever leave him again.
- **The endings:**
  - If you pull the stone out, he's an old man on a cold floor who says he meant to come out to the farm one spring and tell you everything.
  - If you take the city, you become the next M.

## Updates on your home screen
- **Install once:** open the game in your phone's browser and choose **Add to Home Screen**. It opens full screen, upright, with its own icon.
- **Updates arrive by themselves:** each time I push, the app checks for the new version when you open it or switch back to it, and every 10 minutes while it's open.
- **When one is found:** the game saves your farm, a toast tells you, and a **yellow bar** appears at the top. Tap the bar and the new version loads.
- **On the loading screen:** if an update is found there, it applies straight away.
