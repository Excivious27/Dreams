# PART 1 — What changed, and what to test

## Smoother
- Walking into new areas causes much smaller hitches.
- Full map redraws cost about a third less.
- The farm soil only redraws when something changes.

## Money and energy
- **Kill crystals:** after 12 kills on a map you get no crystals, except from:
  - named bosses and mine keepers;
  - story foes;
  - a first battle win;
  - colony quota kills.
- **Capped kills:** these still drop items that an open quest asks for.
- **Rest spots:** they have their own half bar a day, so food and energy perks matter again.
- **Food:** eaten at a full bar, it isn't lost.
- **Bait:** now pulls better fish.
- **Crystal blooms:** 4 a day, down from 10.
- **Wisp drops:** worth less.
- **Gangster lookouts:** now drop loot.
- **Gun damage:** capped. Old saves are refunded for the changed nodes.

## Co-op
- Visiting someone no longer stretches your buff and pup timers by weeks.
- The guest's sprinklers and chests stay on their own farm.
- A guest's morning resets correctly.

## Farm
- Clearing one weed no longer reshuffles all the other debris.
- The greenhouse, windmill and fish pond fill their space, with no invisible walls.
- Snow waters crops.
- Baby animals don't ask for hay.

## Mines
- Barrels are visible.
- The old miner's spot is now a pickaxe stuck in a boulder.
- Deep and crystal floors have lamps.
- Early floors and the keeper are gentler.

## Regions
- **Quarry:** no invisible walls.
- **Clean-up:** frozen stand-in props, blocking flower decals and houses on roads are gone.
- **Countryside:** the highway is real asphalt.
- **Beach:** the sand edge is soft, and crabs live there, so Finn's crab quest works in both arcs.
- **Exits:** the mountain-to-quarry and meadow-to-ruins exits have paths.

## Story
- Main quests open on time. You'll find:
  - the Knight quest on the sky islands;
  - the finale quest on the station;
  - the tower quest after the Enforcer.
- Sasha's scene waits until after the letter doors.
- Heart quests open at 6 hearts, so the 6-heart scene no longer asks for something you already did.
- Catkins shows his own art.
- More lines softened for all ages: the Biscuit collar, Deuce no longer described as a dealer, Tam's sheep line.

## Screen and rooms
- **HUD:**
  - The goal line wraps to two lines.
  - Toasts stay clear of the shop.
  - The boss banner sits above you.
- **Enemies:** chasers walk around walls and wait at the shore while you swim.
- **Interiors:**
  - The bank, mill and stable walls line up.
  - No checkered floors.
  - People aren't hidden behind desks.
  - Windows are clear.

## Art re-cut
- the right mid trees in every season;
- solid crystal enemies;
- crystal babies with their own sprites;
- husky and cat pet poses;
- Ivy's portrait;
- effects and region props;
- fewer stray edge lines on 36 sprites;
- softer cloud shadows.

## What to test (10 minutes)
1. Fight past 12 kills in one area: a toast should tell you kills stop paying.
2. Use the farm well, then eat something. Does the food still give energy?
3. Clear a weed, leave the farm and come back. The rest of the debris should stay put.
4. Walk around the quarry and the countryside.
5. Pick the husky or a cat as your pet and watch it play.
