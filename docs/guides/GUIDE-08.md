## How the workshop works

**Nothing is given.** Every station and building is either built from materials or bought ready-made for crystals, at the **Plans board** next to your house. The board is sorted into shelves: Farm buildings, Workshop stations, Kitchen & cellar, Big projects, and one shelf of your arc's own: The operation (gangsters) or The sanctuary (penguins).

**Every station has four tiers.** A tier opens when three things line up:

| Tier | Story | Skill level | Typical cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | from the start | 1 | raw wood, stone, ore |
| 2 | after the meadow is cleared | 3 | planks, nails, bricks, copper and iron bars |
| 3 | after chapter 2 | 5 | beams, gears, steel, tile |
| 4 | after chapter 3 | 7 | shadowsteel, plating, crystal glass, moonweave |

**What a better tier gives you:**

- **More jobs at once:** 1, 2, 3, then 4 slots.
- **Faster work:** 15% quicker per tier.
- **Higher recipes:** a tier-N station makes recipes up to tier N+1.
- **Masterwork:** from tier 2, a 5%, 10%, then 20% chance of a bonus piece on each batch.

**Stations feed each other.** The lumberyard's planks and the anvil's nails build the tier-2 stations. The kiln's bricks build the better furnace, which smelts the steel the anvil needs for gears and plating, and so on up to the rocket. A check that runs before every push proves there are no dead ends in either arc.

**Using a station:** walk up and press USE. Pick a recipe, pick how many (1, 5, or as many as you can afford), and walk away. Jobs run on the game clock, including while you sleep, and you get a note when one finishes. Locked recipes are listed with what they need ("needs Smithy", "needs Mining Lv 5"). Menu → Quests → **Workshop** shows every station, its tier and its running jobs.

**Town benches:** Gideon's forge and Wren's cauldron work the same way before you own your own. Their tier grows with that villager's hearts (up to tier 3), and each piece costs a small fee.

**Magic Potion Station** potions: Hearth Brew (warmth), Mana Potion, Swift Draught (speed), Luck Potion, Greater Potion (big heal), Stoneskin Tonic (defence), Growth Elixir (every crop on your farm grows a day), and at the top tier the Moonlight Elixir (everything restored).

**Vehicle Workshop.** Hold the vehicle in your toolbar and press USE to ride; USE again to hop off. No tools while riding.

- Kick Scooter: about 1.5× walking speed.
- Rowboat: row across water instead of swimming, fast and with no energy cost.
- Motor Scooter: nearly 2×.
- Crystal Sled (penguin arc): fast everywhere, fastest in winter and on the mountain.
- Getaway Car (gangster arc): the fastest thing on wheels.
- Hot-Air Balloon: USE it outdoors to drift to any outdoor area you've already walked into.

**Rocket Yard.** Four stages: Launch Scaffold, Rocket Frame, Fuelled Rocket, Starship. You build the parts at the yard itself (hull plates, heat shields, engines, a nav computer). From stage 3 it launches from your farm to the moon; stage 4 also reaches the star station. Each launch burns one Fuel Cell. It will only fly somewhere you've already reached on foot once, so it can't skip the story.

## Gangster arc only: the operation

All-ages caper rules: no blood, nothing graphic, nothing mature. Shots are a flash and a puff of dust, and the drinks are soda.

| Station | Tiers | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Back-Room Press | Back-Room Press → Offset Press → Engraving Press → The Mint | Blank Stock, Printing Ink, Crude Bills, Passable Bills, Forged Permit… |
| Street Armory | Trunk of Tools → Back-Alley Armory → Fixer's Workshop → The Vault | Brass Knuckles, Fedora, Crowbar, Lucky Bat, Pinstripe Suit… |
| Soda Fountain | Soda Cart → Soda Fountain → Parlour → The Gold Room | Root Beer, Cream Soda, Cherry Fizz, Golden Float |
| Laundromat | Wash Tub → Laundromat → Cleaners & Sons | Leave up to 3 bills overnight: paid at 80% with no risk |
| Recruit Training Center | Back-Lot Gym → Training Hall → The Academy | Spar once a day for combat XP |

**Printed bills and Heat.** The press prints four grades of bill. Hold a bundle and press USE in any town to pass it for crystals:

| Bill | Pays | Chance it's noticed |
|---|---|---|
| Crude | ◆70 | 30% |
| Passable | ◆150 | 18% |
| Fine | ◆320 | 9% |
| Perfect | ◆700 | 3% |

If a bill is noticed you lose it, pay half its value to smooth things over, a shopkeeper likes you a little less, no till in town will take another bill that day, and your **Heat** goes up by one (0 to 5). Each point of Heat adds 5% to every later bill's risk. At Heat 3 or more, the President's collectors may be waiting when you walk into a street. Heat cools by one each night.

**The Laundromat** is the safe way: leave bills in the wash and they're paid out clean the next morning with no risk, at 80% to 90% of their value, a few per night. Owning one also cools Heat twice as fast.

**Forged Permit:** stands in for a building permit from Alder. One in four is spotted, which costs you a point of Heat.

**Street Armory:** Brass Knuckles, Crowbar, Lucky Bat, Chrome Blade, Neon Cleaver; four guns (Six-Shooter, Scattergun, Tommy Gun, Gold Revolver) that hit at range and use one Round per shot (the armory makes Rounds 30 at a time; with none left the gun is just a weak club); Fedora, Shades, Pinstripe Suit, Padded Vest, Armored Suit, Steel-Toe Shoes, Knuckle Gloves, Trench Coat, Driver's Belt, Gold Chain.

**Recruit Training Center:** spar once a day for combat experience. From tier 2, every recruited crew member's perk counts one loyalty step higher (two at tier 3), and building it raises your max health.

## What to test (15 minutes)

1. **Plans board:** open it by the house. Check the shelves read clearly on your phone, and that a locked tier says why.
2. **First chain:** build the Sawhorse (30 wood, 10 stone). Saw planks, 5 at once. Walk away and wait for the "ready" note, then collect.
3. **Buy instead:** pick a station you can't afford in materials and buy it ready-made.
4. **Keg:** build one, hold any crop, start a brew. It should say how many days.
5. **Crops:** plant a few different seeds and look at the new growth art as days pass.
6. **Menu → Quests → Workshop** and **Field log**: both should be readable.
7. **Penguin arc:** build Big Jim's Campfire and toast a marshmallow; build the Merkitty Pool, sleep, and collect the gift.
8. **Gangster arc** (new game): build the Back-Room Press, print Crude Bills, pass a few in town, and watch Heat in the Workshop page.
