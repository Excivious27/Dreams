## What to test on your phone
- **Switching apps:** start a song, switch to another app and back. The game should go quiet while away and pick up again.
- **Sound page:** on the title screen and in Options, move each volume a few steps; check Reset.
- **Loops:** let a town or farm song loop; there should be no click or dip at the join.
- **Fights:** beat a boss; you should hear one victory sting, then the area song where it left off.
- **Farm:** water until the can is empty, then refill at the river. Mine a rock and chop a tree with the starting tools.
- **Bag:** long-press an item and drag it to another slot, the toolbar, a chest and the trash. Try Organise.
- **Places:** walk on a pier or bridge (wood steps), wade (splash), go into a cave (no birds). Visit the festival at night.
- **Arcade:** a winning round plays a happy sting, not the losing one. Try the home Arcade Cabinet and the Music Box.
