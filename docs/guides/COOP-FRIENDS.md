# Play Dreams with friends (phones)

Everything here is done on phones. There is nothing to set up on a computer: the Dreams server is already running and the game uses it by itself.

## Before you start

- Send friends the play link ahead of time: https://excivious27.github.io/Dreams/ . The first open downloads about 180 MB, so do it on Wi-Fi. After that it opens fast.
- Everyone opens the link. If a yellow "New version of Dreams ready" bar shows, tap it now, before anyone hosts or joins, so everyone is on the same version. Nobody taps it during a session (it reloads the page and drops that player).
- A friend new to Dreams taps New game and makes a character (any story path). They keep this character, skills and bag.
- iPhone friends: pick Safari or the Home Screen icon and stay with it. Each keeps its own character.
- One player per phone. A second tab on the same phone shares that phone's farm, so it doesn't work as a second player.
- Leave the Server box alone. If anyone changed it, tap Reset to default.
- Pick the farm to share in Save slots before you host: your current slot is the world everyone visits.
- Talk on a call or in a group text while you play: there is no chat in the game (all ages), only friendly preset emotes where they exist.

## Hosting and joining

- Host (you): Title > Play together > Host a room. Read the 4-letter code to your friends (or share it). Codes never use O/0 or I/1.
- Friends: Title > Play together, type the code in Room code, tap Join with code. Everyone hears a chime and sees "Kai joined." (with the friend's name).
- Start with one friend, then add more. A room holds up to 8 players.
- Host on Wi-Fi if you can; friends are fine on mobile data.
- Keep the host phone plugged in with Dreams on screen. If the host's screen locks or the host switches apps for more than about half a minute, the room ends and everyone goes back to their own farm. If your phone still locks on its own, set Auto-Lock to 5 minutes or Never while you play (iPhone: Settings > Display & Brightness > Auto-Lock; Android: Settings > Display > Screen timeout).

## How a shared game works

- Your farm is the world: crops, buildings, animals, the clock, weather, difficulty and day length are yours.
- Each friend keeps their own character, money, skills, bag and quests, and everything they earn goes home with them.
- A friend's own farm waits while they visit: no days pass there and nothing wilts. Closing Dreams in the middle of a visit is safe: their own farm is saved, and next time they open on their own farm, not on yours.
- Places a friend sees on your world stay a visit: they don't open new regions, travel lines or story borders in their own game.
- Different story paths can play together: each player sees their own story scenes, and some places look different.
- Friends: the difficulty and day length you picked in New game don't matter here; the host's count.
- Night: friends tap their bed; the day ends when the host sleeps. A host night refreshes friends for your day, but when they go home their own day carries on where they left it (no free extra day).
- If a friend faints, they wake up in your farmhouse, with no clinic bill.
- When the host opens the menu, everyone's clock waits.

## Leaving and coming back

- Leave with Menu > Options > Save and back to title. Everyone else hears a door sound and sees "Name left."
- When the host leaves, every friend goes back to their own farm with a message.
- A friend whose phone locks for a short while usually reconnects by itself when it's unlocked ("Connection lost, reconnecting..."). If they end up back on their own farm, they rejoin from Title > Play together with the same code.
- The code only works while someone is still in the room.

## Steam (desktop build, later)

- In the Steam version, Play together also has "Host with Steam friends". Friends join from your Steam friends list (Join Game) or from the invite. There's no code for a Steam room.
- Steam friends and phone friends can't be in the same room. For a mixed group, host with a room code (Phones and web).

## Known limits right now

- Friends can't start battle campaigns, the drill or the long way on your farm; they do those at home.
- You clear your own community-farm lot.
- Your difficulty and day length count for everyone.
- Friends can look at your house but not move your furniture.
- There is no chat in the game, by design.
- The Steam friends button only shows in the Steam version.
- If the host's phone sleeps for more than about half a minute, the room ends.

## What to test with friends

Each line says what should happen. Put what you saw in the "Co-op tests" tab of the progress spreadsheet (Result column).

- Host a room: the 4-letter code shows and stays readable.
- Friend joins: chime and "Name joined." on both phones; you see each other walk, with outfits and pets.
- Wrong code: "No game with that code. Check the code with your host."
- Ninth player: "That room is full (8 players)."
- Different paths (one penguin save, one gangster save): a short "other story" message; both can play.
- Farm together: a friend waters, plants and harvests your crops; you see it within a couple of seconds.
- Host opens the menu: the friend's clock stops; close it and the clock goes on.
- Night: the friend taps bed, the host sleeps, everyone wakes on the new day.
- Friend in the mines or forest while the host is on the farm: both play, no jumping back.
- Friend tries a battle campaign, drill or long way: a polite "wait until you're home" message.
- Friend's phone locks for 10 seconds: it reconnects by itself on unlock.
- Friend closes Dreams in the middle of a visit and opens it again: they start on their own farm, with their own day, money and bag.
- Friend leaves with Save and back to title: their own farm, money and bag are right, and what they earned is kept.
- Host leaves: friends go home (note how long it takes).
- Phone feel: walking looks smooth on 4G and on weak Wi-Fi; note any jumps.
