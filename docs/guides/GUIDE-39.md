## How to use the new things (plain words)

### Writing and story (new this round)

**Story scenes know your hero.** Play as Nova, Pip-Pip, Big Jim, Coldy or Mer and the story speaks to you as that hero: Pip-Pip meets his Grandad, Nova's dream is hers, and nobody calls a penguin "human". At the meadow one of the penguins flies with you if they are your partner.

**Choices come back.** What you pick is answered later. Tell the town about the Grumps or keep it quiet, and Alder, Theo and Maple talk about it afterwards. Your answer to the crystal shows up in later scenes and the ending. Keep the brass locket and Finn helps you take it home to Tomas. In the gangster arc, crew members you kept loyal get their own lines in the finale, and the Clean Break ending carries them too. A friend who turned grey comes back in both penguin endings.

**Festival days.** On a festival day everyone in town gathers on the plaza from 9 to 6, and villagers talk about that festival (ribbons only at the Harvest Fair). If a host has left town, someone else runs the stall. The star wish and lantern float know if it's still daylight. The egg hunt really has 12 eggs. Villagers also have lines for rain, storms, fog, snow, morning, evening and night.

**Side stories in your journal.** MENU > Quests has a new "Side stories" list: each open one, who to see and where. When you finish a step, the hint names the next person. Tap a side story to track it: the corner compass points to that person.

**Letters from people in town.** Letters now match what has happened. Someone who has left town doesn't write, and someone else covers for them. Tam doesn't write from the highlands while he is standing in town. Prices in favour letters show in your arc's money ($ or ◆). Letters are signed. After the penguin ending a few friends write to you, one at a time.

**Gift refusal messages.** A villager who already has a gift from you today says so ("already has a gift from you today"), and so does one who has had two this week. The item stays in your bag. Mega-region locals just chat, so you never lose an item to someone who can't take it.

**Small things.** Every item has a description now. Decor pieces have their own lines and sets end in "Set". Drinks say Drink, not Eat. Hints and menus name your pad's buttons on a controller. Tess has a heart story and a 6-heart job (8 Hardwood for a window seat). Shop deals in the gangster arc never send your partner away.

### Steam prep (from CONDUCT S1)

**Achievements.** The title screen has a new button, "Achievements n/60". It lists all 60: unlocked ones first, progress like 12/50, and secret ones as ??? with a hint. Achievements count across all your farms, and old saves unlock what they have already earned the next time they save. A small "Achievement: ..." note pops up when you earn one. On Steam they will show as Steam achievements too.

**Desktop options (computer only).** Title > Sound and options (or Options in the game) has new rows on a computer: fullscreen or window (F11 or Alt+Enter also switch), Scaling (smooth or pixel-perfect), VSync, "Frame rate cap", and "Pause when window inactive". The game remembers your window size and place. Phones and the web game don't show these rows.

**Typing with a controller.** On a name box press A: a keyboard opens on screen (A types a letter, B deletes or closes, MENU is done). On a Steam Deck, Steam's own keyboard will open instead. With a pad, buttons are named the pad's way (A, B, Menu, L1, R1 or the Deck's names), never "tap". Backups on a pad or Deck open as a list (A loads, B cancels) instead of a file window.

**Steam Deck screens.** At the Deck's 1280x800 every word is at least Valve's 9 px size, and fullscreen fills the Deck screen.

**Credits and licences.** Title > Sound and options > Credits and licences: who made the game, how the art and music were made, and the notices for Godot, the fonts and the Steam plug-in.

**Play together.** The live server is the default: Title > Play together, then host or join with a room code. In the Steam build there will also be a "Steam friends" part: "Host with Steam friends", then invite friends from Steam (or they join from your friends list); the room code stays under "Phones and web (room code)". Everyone needs the same game version: an older version gets a clear "update first" message.

**Beasts run off.** Beaten wolves, hounds, crabs, serpents, eels and rocs now hop back and run away. They never drop meat (the old pelt is now "Shed Fur").


### Ranching part 2 (from CONDUCT 11b)

**Exotic animals (both arcs) and where to buy them.** Each needs the right home first:

- Peacock: Hazel, once you have an Aviary.
- Tortoise: Posy, once you have a Terrarium.
- Alpaca: Tam, once you have the Big Barn.

The Sun Drake (penguin arc) / Komodo dragon (gangster arc) is never sold: it comes from a quest (not wired yet). Exotics give big goods: giant eggs, plumes, fine fleece, shed scales, yak wool and buffalo milk.

**Creatures you earn (never sold).** When one is ready for you, a letter says so. If you don't have room, it waits and the letter says which home or tier makes room; it arrives the morning after you build it.
- Colonies (penguin arc): bring a colony's light back and the elder sends a creature: Coral Seahorse (Coral Reach), Kelp Calf (Kelp Hollow), Frost Otter (Frostfin Isle), Ember Newt (Emberclaw Atoll).
- Mega regions: Liesl's job in Gloamwood gives a Lantern Moth (gangster arc: a peacock); Nadia's job in Sunscorch gives an ostrich; Ingrid's job in Frostpeak gives a snow yak; Nimbus's job in Windward gives a Cloud Sheep (gangster arc: an emu).
- Bosses: after the Windward boss, the Storm Roc nest at Starfall gives a young Storm Roc (penguin arc). After the Frost Summit boss you get a Frost Wyrmling egg (gangster arc: a tortoise). The Hollow Deeps gives a Deep Glowworm (gangster arc: a volt gecko).
- The Moon (penguin arc): after Gideon's moon job, tap the moon cow and she comes home.
- Friends: Hazel at 10 hearts gives a golden hen (Saffron), Posy at 6 hearts a silver rabbit (Crumpet), Benno at 8 hearts a chocolate goat kid (Tinker).
- Side stories: Mama Rosa's restaurant gives 2 kitchen hens, The Kid's school a class rabbit (Professor), Coldy's key a snow duck (Frost).
- Tam's lamb Bramble still comes at 8 hearts; if your barn is full she squeezes in anyway.
- Still to come: Crystal Hen, Star Sheep, Moon Hare, Star Jelly, Glow Gecko and Aurora Fox have pictures and homes but no giver yet.

**New homes.** On the Plans board, animal homes now have their own page ("Ranch (animal homes)", or "Livestock" in the gangster arc). Each shows how many live there, what it has, and what the next tier adds.
- Exotic Enclosure (2) and Grand Enclosure (4, incubator, babies, heater).
- Aviary (4, perches) and Grand Aviary (8, feeder, incubator, heater).
- Terrarium (2, heater) and Grand Terrarium (4, babies, water).
- Tide Pool (sea creatures), Ember Pit (warm rocks, newts), Star Dome (penguin arc: Moon and far-north creatures).
- Grand Barn: the barn's 4th tier, room for 16 with big stalls.
The hutch and sty now stand in the pasture east of the farm track, each with its pen in front of the door. Gangster names: Exotics Yard, Drone Loft, Grow Tank, Freight Barn.

**Food and comfort.** Some creatures eat their own food instead of hay (the hint says "hold Moonberry"). Some need a home add-on, or warmth in winter: without it they give no goods, and the animal card says why.

**Gene Splicer (gangster arc).** Build it at the operation: Splice Rig, then Gene Splicer, then Gene Lab. Put in goods and circuit boards (data chips for the big ones) to splice a lab strain: chrome hen and turbo bunny (Splice Rig); glow sheep, circuit goat and circuit cow (Gene Splicer); drone hog, volt gecko and chrome runner (Gene Lab). The animal comes home like a gift. All friendly: chrome, glowing wool and soft status lights, never anything scary.

**Breeding.** Rare looks now have names ("a rare golden hen!"), shown with a star on the Animals page. Tap an animal there to see its parents, kids, generation and birthday. The hint says "expecting: 3 days" before a baby, and the morning report says "comes tonight". Rare animals sometimes give better goods. The Ranching tree's Rare Bloodlines node makes rares twice as common.

**Shows, ribbons and medals.** At the Harvest Fair choose the Animal Show (Stock Show in the gangster arc) or the Ranch Goods table. Spring has the Best Egg table, summer's Lantern festival has Fleece & Feather. The judges look at hearts, mood, star quality, rare looks and how long the animal has been grown, never price. 1st, 2nd and 3rd win money and a blue, red or yellow ribbon to hang at home; 4th gets an honourable mention. Three blue ribbons win the trophy, and Collections gives a bronze medal, silver medal and crystal trophy at 1, 3 and 8 blue ribbons. Rivals get a little better each year. Your animal comes home proud (friendship up).

**Chores and helper limits.** The Chores page shows how many helper spots you have (3 at first, up to 5 as the game goes on; Farm Hands don't count) and tonight's haul cap. Helpers stop when the night's haul is worth the cap ("the rest waits for you"), so they help without doing everything. Each helper's energy grows with your bond and its chore level, and at 24 energy it can do a second chore. Feed takes hay from the home's trough, then the silo, then the chest you pick. Forage lets you pick where they go ("Forages at: ..."). Guard keeps crows off the beds near them. A helper's pet counts half; your own pet still counts.

**Team animals and lessons.** Walk up to a trainable pen animal and press CHECK: Teach, Team, Pet or Close. Each Teach button says what's left ("rank 1 · 3 more to rank 2", "tomorrow", "needs a treat"). Lessons rank up at 1, 4 and 7. Guard now shoves foes back; Fetch carries finds back to your feet; team animals keep up when you run. Before a battle, the briefing lets you tap to pick 4 for your team; team animals line up first.

**Battle training (fixed).** "Leave training" sits in the corner of every step's card; it asks first, and B keeps training. On a pad, the pause menu's Items tab has "End training". Leaving takes you back to the campaign map with Training ready to try again. Helpers now follow you from the start. The HELPERS button (L3 on a pad) gives plain orders: "Follow me", "Hold here" or "Go to" a place. Helpers are built at a banner: "Build helper: Spark Crystal (◇20)" is first on the list. The paw buttons on the left are your pup's, not the helpers'. Training is 8 short steps, one order each.

**Pup and partners.** Pup commands say one thing each. Fetch really fetches forage now. When the pup is resting, the buttons are greyed and say when it's back. Partner powers cool down on game time and show a ring. A ♥ tap while you're busy is ignored.

**Voices.** Every new creature has its own gentle voice, and lab strains add a soft beep. Coops, barns and the new homes play a countryside song inside.


### Ranching part 1 (from CONDUCT 11a)

**The animals.** Chickens (Hens in the gangster arc), ducks, cows, sheep, goats, llamas, pigs (Hogs), rabbits and bees. Each lives in its own home: chickens and ducks in the coop, cows, sheep, goats and llamas in the barn, rabbits in the hutch, pigs in the sty, bees in the beehive.

**Buying animals.** Tam's shop has "Animals for your ranch": sheep, goats, llamas and alpacas. You need the right home with room, and some animals need a bigger home first (goats, llamas and ducks need tier 2). Hazel sells chickens, ducks, cows and pigs, Posy sells rabbits and bees (pigs now need the Big Sty).

**Homes and tiers.** Build them from the Plans board with materials:
- Coop: Coop (4 birds), Big Coop (8, feeder and incubator, Building 3), Deluxe Coop (12, auto-water and heater, Ranching 5).
- Barn: Barn (4), Big Barn (8, feeder, animals can have babies, Building 5), Deluxe Barn (12, auto-water and heater, Ranching 7).
- Rabbit Hutch (learn the Hutch Blueprint in the Ranching tree) and Big Hutch (Ranching 4).
- Pig Sty (learn the Sty Blueprint) and Big Sty (Ranching 6).
- Beehive, Bee House (Ranching 3), Apiary (Ranching 6).
- Silo, Tall Silo (Ranching 2), Twin Silo (Ranching 5): 240, 480 and 720 hay.
Each home has a fenced pen with a gate. Animals go inside at night and in storms or snow, and come back out in the morning. Buildings have their own snow and night looks.

**Feeding, grazing and the silo.** On a dry day outside winter, grown animals graze in their pen and need no hay. On wet days and in winter they need hay: hold hay and tap the animal, or let the feeder in a Big Coop or Big Barn take hay from the silo (milkers are fed first). Scythe long grass for hay, or buy it by the handful. Tap the silo to see how much is left; tap again to take 20 out. When you arrive on the farm a note says how many the silo fed and who went hungry.

**Petting and friendship.** Tap an animal once a day to pet it. The hint tells you what it needs: "A: Clover (hungry)", "pet me", "goods ready" or "happy". A good care day is a pet and a meal, in either order. Friendship goes up to 10 hearts; a missed day costs a little but never resets everything. Animals never get sick and never die: a hungry animal just looks sad until you feed it.

**Goods and quality.** Goods are ready in the morning: eggs, duck eggs, milk, goat milk, wool (sheep and llamas), rabbit fur, truffles (pigs dig them outdoors, not in winter or rain), honey and beeswax, and feathers from roosters. Happier, better-loved animals give silver and gold star goods and bigger eggs and milk. Tap the animal to collect; anything left from untended days is collected on your next tap. The morning report has one "Ranch" block with what's ready, who was hungry, births and hatches.

**Breeding and the incubator.** Any two grown, happy animals of the same kind in a home that allows babies (Big Barn, hutch, Big Sty, Bee House) may have a baby overnight if there's room. No gender checks. Babies sometimes come in a rare colour. In the Big Coop, hold an egg and tap the incubator by the coop: hen eggs hatch in 3 days, duck eggs in 4. Babies grow up in about a week.

**Names.** New animals get a name; rename them on the Animals page (Party tab).

**Rehoming (never sold for meat).** On the Animals page you can rehome an animal: it goes to live on Tam's high pasture and you get a small thank-you (nothing for very young ones). No animal is ever sold for meat or harmed. Bramble can't be rehomed: she's a keepsake.

**The Ranching skill tree.** The 14th tree, in both arcs, with four branches: Produce (more goods, a chance of a second good, better prices), Quality and Care (stars sooner, missed days forgiven), Breeding (more babies, faster growing, a warmer incubator), and Friends and Helpers (petting counts double sometimes, Farm Hands). It also teaches the Hutch and Sty Blueprints. Care, petting and collecting give Ranching XP. Old saves with animals got some Ranching XP to start.

**New stations and goods.** Mayo Machine (eggs to mayonnaise, duck mayonnaise) and Honey Extractor (honeycomb to honey and beeswax, jars of honey, beeswax candles), each with 2 tiers. The cheese press also makes cream, honey yogurt and truffle oil. The loom makes yarn from 1 wool + 1 fiber (2 yarn) and from rabbit fur, and a Down Pillow from feathers and cloth. Honey in a keg makes Flower Mead (a trade good only, nobody drinks it). Starred goods keep their star when you process them. The Book has a new Ranch log.

**Team animals and lessons.** A grown animal at 2 hearts can learn lessons: on the Animals page, tap Teach with one of its treats (carrot, apple, hay, wheat or berry). One lesson a day, 8 energy. Lessons: Charge (butts foes), Guard (holds its ground beside you), Fetch (brings forage), Carry (hauls the harvest). After a lesson it can join your team (up to 2). Team animals follow you outdoors, help in fights and battle campaigns, and show on the Hero tab's Team card. If one gets knocked down it rests in its pen until the next day.

**Overnight chores.** From day 8, the Chores page (Party tab) lets your pup, team animals, crew and partner do one chore each night: water crops, harvest ripe crops into a chest, feed the animals, keep them company, or forage the edges into a chest. Each helper has energy and gets better with practice (5 levels). The Ranching tree's Farm Hands add two more helpers for feeding and petting. The morning report says what they did; a full chest is reported and nothing is lost.

**Bramble.** Reach 8 hearts with Tam and he promises you a lamb. Once your barn has room, Bramble arrives (rename her if you like). After 10 hearts Tam writes four letters from the highlands, and asks you to bring her to the Harvest Fair.

**Animal Show and Best Egg.** At the Harvest Fair (the Block Fair livestock ribbon in the gangster arc), enter a grown animal (friendship and mood count most) or your best animal good. At the Egg Festival, the Best Egg table takes your best egg. Prizes are money; your animal goes home after the show.

**Market stalls.** On the plaza, Benno's feed stall (9 to 11) and Hazel's farm stall (8 to 11) sell hay, seed and fertiliser. Shut on festival days.

**Letters and villagers.** Hazel (Benno in the gangster arc) writes about animals around days 6 to 14, welcome letters come with a small gift when you build or upgrade a coop or barn, and you get a letter for your first egg, milk and wool. Favour letters only ask for animal goods you can actually make. Your spouse feeds every animal on chore days.

### From CONDUCT 10b (still the same)

**Restyling the farmhouse.** Tap the Plans board and pick "Restyle the farmhouse". The camera frames your house. Tabs along the top: Roof, Walls, Windows, Stonework, Door, Shutters, Porch, Mailbox, Pen fence. Tap < and > to try styles; the house changes live. Buy pays once; after that Apply switches for free. Original puts the first look back. Closing without buying keeps your saved look.

**Decorating the yard.** Near your house, tap "Decorate yard" (bottom left), or use the Plans board. It works like decorating indoors: green ghost fits, red with a cross doesn't, and the reason shows. Paths and small pieces can be painted by dragging. You can't build on soil, doors, exits, the river, pens or building sites. If you start a building where yard decor stands, the decor goes back to the box.

**Tess's shop.** Tess is the carpenter in town. She writes to you around day 3. Her workshop sells furniture and yard pieces, and the shelves change every few days. Gangster crew counters (Marlene, Deuce, Specs, Halo) sell their own pieces too. Ordering a piece you've seen costs 10% extra (at least 10), delivered next morning.

**The casino and its limits (gangster arc).** The Gilded Ace is on Casino Row in Gilt City, open 6pm to 2am. Five tables: slots, roulette, Big/Small dice, blackjack and video poker. Every table tells you exactly how much the house keeps; every bet returns less than 99% over time, so the house always wins in the long run. Each table has a free practice hand. Limits: a $500 daily loss limit (on by default), a cap on hands per day, a reality check, and "Take a break" (7 days, can't be undone). Big wins add heat; with heat 3 or more the doorman turns you away. Keepsakes come for trying tables, never for winning money. The Odds board on the Row shows every table's numbers.

**The wardrobe on every hero.** At the dresser at home, or Hero tab > Wardrobe. Hats and face pieces show on every drawn hero, and capes show on everyone. Pieces that can't be drawn on your hero say so. Sets show a Claim button when complete. In co-op your friend sees your outfit.
