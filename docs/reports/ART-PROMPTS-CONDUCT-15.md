Prompt (all, with the object word swapped): "Redraw this exact game prop (same object, same angle, same proportions, same colours and pixel-art-like painted style) as its WINTER version: a soft layer of fresh snow on the top surfaces, a little frost on the edges and windows, a few icicles where it fits. Look: checked side by side with the originals and in-game (Hideout winter/fall, Neon winter/fall). Known small mismatches: lux_car_black twin is a slightly different car angle; water_tower legs are wood (original red steel); motel twin lacks the original's car-park strip; billboard twin is blank. Acceptable for winter but listed for a later re-render. Follow-up art list in docs/ART-QUEUE.md.
