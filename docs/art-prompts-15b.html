REDO — The operation, as sprites (2 images)

Make TWO SEPARATE IMAGES, 1536×1024 each. These are GAME SPRITE SHEETS, not posters, infographics or catalogue pages. Match the attached sheet exactly: chunky 16-bit pixel art, top-down 3/4 view, clean 1px dark outlines, warm Stardew Valley / Sun Haven palette.

HARD RULES: flat #FF00FF magenta fills the whole image edge to edge. NO title banners. NO captions, labels, numbers or any letters at all. NO panels, frames, borders or rounded boxes. NO room interiors: no walls, floors, ceilings or backgrounds behind anything. Every item is ONE free-standing object or small building seen from OUTSIDE, sitting alone on magenta with at least 24 px of clear magenta around it, exactly like the stations in the attached sheet. Nothing touches anything else.
